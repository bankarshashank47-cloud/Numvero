Keyboard shortcuts

Press Enter to calculate, Backspace to delete, or use number keys and operators.

How to Use

  1. Click the number buttons or type numbers on your keyboard
  2. Select an operation: +, −, ×, ÷, %
  3. Enter another number
  4. Press = or Enter to calculate
  5. Use AC to clear and start over, or ⌫ to delete one digit

Examples

Addition: 25 + 15 = 40

Subtraction: 100 − 30 = 70

Multiplication: 12 × 8 = 96

Division: 150 ÷ 5 = 30

Percentage: 20% of 500 = 100

Brackets: (10 + 5) × 2 = 30
