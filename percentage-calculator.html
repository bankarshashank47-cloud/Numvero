Percentage Calculator

Calculate what percentage one number is of another, or find a percentage of a total amount. Perfect for discounts, tips, grades, and more.

—

Percentage Formula

Percentage of a number: (Percentage ÷ 100) × Total = Result

Example: 25% of 800 = (25 ÷ 100) × 800 = 200

How to Use

  1. Enter the percentage value in the first field (e.g., 25)
  2. Enter the total amount in the second field (e.g., 800)
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the result

Common Uses

Discount: 20% off $50 = $10 discount

Tip: 15% tip on $120 = $18 tip

Grade: 85% of 100 questions = 85 correct

Increase: 10% raise on $50,000 = $5,000 increase
