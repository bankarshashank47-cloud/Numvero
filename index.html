FAQ

Common questions

Are Numvero calculators free?

Yes. The calculators are free to use and do not require an account.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open Numvero in a modern browser. The calculator interface works on desktop, tablet and mobile.

Do calculations leave my browser?

The calculator logic runs in your browser for normal calculations. Numvero does not require sign-in to calculate.

Can I use keyboard input?

Yes. The basic and scientific calculators support keyboard entry, Enter to calculate and Escape to close.
