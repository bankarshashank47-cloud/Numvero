Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. The calculators are designed to work directly in your browser.

Does the calculator work on mobile phones?

Can I use the scientific calculator for trigonometry?

How is BMI calculated?

Metric BMI is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared.
