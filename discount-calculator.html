Discount Calculator

Calculate the final price after applying a percentage discount. Perfect for shopping, sales, and special offers.

—

Discount Formula

Final Price: Original Price × (1 − Discount% ÷ 100)

Example: $1000 with 20% discount = $1000 × 0.8 = $800

Amount Saved: Original Price − Final Price

How to Use

  1. Enter the original price in the first field
  2. Enter the discount percentage in the second field
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the final price

Example Scenarios

50% Off: $200 with 50% off = $100

25% Off: $80 with 25% off = $60

Flash Sale: $399 with 30% off = $279.30

Coupon: $150 with 15% off = $127.50
